# The Gaming Industry in New Zealand - Presentation

A professional, interactive HTML presentation about New Zealand's gaming industry, designed for high school and university audiences.

## 📊 Presentation Overview

- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Slides:** 10 slides covering the complete gaming industry landscape
- **Style:** Modern, gaming-themed design with neon aesthetics
- **Features:** Interactive charts, smooth transitions, speaker notes

## 🎮 Content Structure

1. **Title Slide** - Industry overview and key statistics
2. **Introduction** - Global context and NZ's position
3. **Growth Timeline** - Revenue growth 2018-2025 with interactive chart
4. **Grinding Gear Games** - Path of Exile franchise success
5. **Ninja Kiwi** - Bloons franchise and mobile gaming
6. **PikPok** - Mobile gaming powerhouse
7. **Black Salt Games** - Indie success story (DREDGE)
8. **Economic Impact** - Job creation and contributions
9. **Government Support** - Tax rebates and development programs
10. **Future Potential** - Opportunities in VR, AR, AI, and esports
11. **Conclusion** - Summary and closing statement

## 🚀 Features

### Navigation
- **Arrow Keys:** ← Previous slide, → Next slide
- **Space:** Next slide
- **Home/End:** Jump to first/last slide
- **Mouse:** Click navigation buttons at the bottom
- **Touch:** Swipe left/right on mobile devices

### Interactive Elements
- **Progress Bar:** Visual indicator at the top
- **Slide Counter:** Shows current slide number
- **Charts:** Interactive revenue growth and economic impact visualizations
- **Speaker Notes:** Click "📝 Notes" button to view presentation notes

### Special Controls
- **A Key:** Toggle auto-advance mode (30 seconds per slide)
- **F11:** Toggle fullscreen presentation mode

## 🎨 Design Features

- Dark gaming-inspired color scheme
- Neon blue (#00fff7) and purple (#b026ff) accents
- Custom fonts: Orbitron (headings) and Rajdhani (body)

## 🖥️ How to Use

### Opening the Presentation
1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari recommended
3. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

### Presenting
1. Press **F11** for fullscreen mode
2. Click "📝 Notes" to open speaker notes panel
3. Use arrow keys or navigation buttons to advance
4. Optional: Press **A** to enable auto-advance (demos/practice)

### For Best Results
- Use a display resolution of 1920x1080 or higher
- Ensure stable internet connection for fonts to load
- Test presentation before live delivery
- Practice with speaker notes open
